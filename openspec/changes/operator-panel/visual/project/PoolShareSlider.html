Two widths of the settings column: 360 px (desktop, 25rem aside minus 2 x 20 px padding) and 300 px (phone), each the width of the content inside the card. The frames share one value: what you do in one shows in the other. Save simulates 700 ms of saving. The first read of the amount takes 400 ms; a typed decimal waits 300 ms after the last keystroke. The two information buttons start closed and are not reset by this bar. The heading and the pairs stub are stand-ins in English.
